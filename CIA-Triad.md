# CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is one of the core concepts in cybersecurity.

## Confidentiality

Confidentiality means protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access.

Examples:

* Password protection
* Encryption
* Multi-factor authentication

## Integrity

Integrity ensures data is accurate and not modified improperly.

Examples:

* File hashing
* Digital signatures
* Access controls

## Availability

Availability ensures systems and data are accessible when needed.

Examples:

* Backups
* Disaster recovery
* Redundant systems

## Real-World Example

A hospital system must protect patient records:

* Confidentiality → Only authorized staff can view records
* Integrity → Records cannot be altered improperly
* Availability → Doctors must access records anytime

## Why It Matters

The CIA Triad helps organizations build strong cybersecurity strategies and reduce risk.
